# Product

This mobile UI and brand brief is derived from the root `PRD.md`. It does not
own game rules, protocol fields, milestone scope, or implementation status.

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Workshop organizers create and localize a shared physical arena. Participants
answer a quiz, lock a stationary position, then rotate and aim through their
iOS or Android camera during a short colocated battle.

## Product Purpose

CodexWars makes quiz performance immediately meaningful by turning earned
powers into a reliable, server-authoritative AR battle. Success is a complete
organizer-to-winner flow that works consistently across physical iPhones and
ARCore-capable Android phones.

## Positioning

Kahoot meets laser tag: a classroom quiz becomes a shared physical finale
without camera upload, body recognition, or cloud spatial anchors.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, welcoming, and dependable. The interface should feel competitive
without weapon realism and playful without looking childish.

## Anti-references

Avoid military imagery, gun-shaped controls, neon sci-fi clutter, tiny HUD
labels, decorative glass panels, and visual effects that obscure the real room
or reduce situational awareness.

## Design Principles

- Physical safety and tracking confidence are always visible.
- One primary action advances each setup step.
- AR is presentation; server state remains authoritative.
- Platform conventions and large touch targets outrank decorative novelty.
- Fantasy combat feedback must remain readable against unpredictable camera backgrounds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast scrims over camera content, minimum 48 dp / 44 pt touch
targets, text plus shape for status, sound/haptic companions for hits, and
reduced-motion-safe transitions. Organizer controls must support quiz-only
participants who cannot enter AR combat.
